# Memo for Finance: what we can count on

*As of 2026-10-21. Prepared for the CFO and the Finance team. Source: the claims table only.*

## The answer in three lines

1. **Counted and safe to report: $1,945,489** paid on 97 claims. The insurers reduced those claims by a further $636,901, and denied 49 claims worth $1,123,431.
2. **Still to be decided: $2,225,289 asked on 90 open claims.** We expect about **$1.17M** to be approved (likely between $1.04M and $1.30M). The earlier Flash figure of $1.68M assumed no claim is ever denied, so treat it as the most it could be, not what to plan on.
3. **Held out for checking: 4 claims** have impossible amounts or payment dates. They are excluded from the paid figure until someone reviews them.

Every dollar is accounted for: the groups add up to $6,066,812 submitted across 250 claims.

## Please read before quoting a number

- **The 90 claims with no approved amount are not lost data.** They are claims the insurer has not decided yet. A blank is normal until then.
- **78 of the 90 open claims were submitted more than a year ago.** If they are no longer being chased, the expected figure is too high.
- **The brief's $2.3M and $1.8M cannot be reproduced** from the data. Use the figures above and say where they come from.
- **Dates cannot tell us when money arrived:** 84 of the 97 paid claims have a date in the wrong order.

## Do not refund yet

We cannot say whether patients were over-charged. The link from each claim to its bill points at the wrong patient on 250 of 250 claims, so any comparison would look exact and be wrong. Hold refund decisions until the link is fixed and the check is re-run.

## What we need from Finance

1. Confirm the paid total against the insurer remittance or the bank.
2. Tell us whether the old open claims are still being worked.
3. Review the 4 held claims (list in the workbook, sheet *DQ Exceptions*).
