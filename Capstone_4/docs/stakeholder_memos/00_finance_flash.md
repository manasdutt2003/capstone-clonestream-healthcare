## Read this before you quote a number

1. **The 'missing approved amounts' are not lost data.** 90 claims have a blank approved amount; all of them are still Submitted, Pending or In Review. A blank is expected until the insurer replies.
2. **2 claims have impossible amounts** (a negative submitted amount, or approved above submitted). They are included above *as recorded*; the Wednesday workbook holds them out separately.
3. **The brief's $2.3M / $1.8M do not match the data.** The data says $2,604,465 / $2,025,699. Use the data's figures.
4. **We cannot yet say whether patients were over-charged.** The link from claims to bills is wrong on every row (rule A02), so any claim-vs-bill comparison would look precise and be wrong.

*Confidence: Certain = read directly from a decided claim. Estimated = depends on an insurer decision that has not happened.*
